Penyelesaian Soal No. 7:
Jumlah sudut segitiga = $180^\circ \Rightarrow \angle C = 180^\circ - (45^\circ + 75^\circ) = 60^\circ$.
Dengan Aturan Sinus: $\frac{AB}{\sin 60^\circ} = \frac{BC}{\sin 45^\circ} \Rightarrow \frac{AB}{\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{12}}{\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}} \Rightarrow AB = \frac{\sqrt{36}}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{6}{\sqrt{2}} = \mathbf{3\sqrt{2}}$ (Jawaban B).
